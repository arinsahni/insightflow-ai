# InsightFlow AI — Product Requirements

## Product

InsightFlow AI is a Voice of Customer and Product Analytics Copilot for product
managers and analysts.

## Problem

Feedback analysis is often a manual sequence of spreadsheet cleaning, tagging,
counting, quote collection, charting, and recommendation writing. The target
workflow makes this work repeatable and evidence-backed.

## Users

Primary users are product managers and product analysts. Secondary users include
founders, customer success teams, UX researchers, growth teams, and business
analysts.

## Outcome

Users should be able to move from a feedback CSV to validated themes, prioritized
problems, source quotes, recommendations, and experiment proposals. AI supports
judgment; it does not make final roadmap decisions.

## Phase 1 scope

Phase 1 includes the repository foundation, environment configuration, navigation
shell, seven safe page states, documentation, and a fictional sample dataset.
It excludes processing, analytics, Gemini calls, and persistence.

## Success measures

Future measured outcomes include time to analyze 500 reviews, classification and
sentiment accuracy, quote-grounding accuracy, evidence coverage, task completion,
trust, and export usage. No target is reported as achieved without measurement.
