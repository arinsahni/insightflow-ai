MASTER BUILD PROMPT — INSIGHTFLOW AI

You are a senior full-stack engineer, data scientist, product analyst, AI engineer, and UX designer.

Your task is to build a complete, polished, deployment-ready project called:

INSIGHTFLOW AI
Voice of Customer and Product Analytics Copilot

The application must help product managers and product analysts convert raw customer feedback into measurable product insights, prioritized problems, evidence-backed recommendations, and A/B testing ideas.

The project must run smoothly on a MacBook with Apple Silicon M4.

Build this project as a practical one-day MVP. Prioritize reliability, clarity, product relevance, and a polished working demo over unnecessary technical complexity.

Do not build a basic sentiment-analysis dashboard. Build a product decision-support system.

==================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT PROBLEM
==================================================

Product teams receive customer feedback from multiple sources such as:

- Google Play reviews
- App Store reviews
- Support tickets
- Customer interviews
- Sales notes
- Surveys
- Social media comments
- Email feedback

Product managers manually read this feedback, categorize complaints, count recurring issues, identify trends, find representative quotes, and decide what should be prioritized.

This workflow is:

- slow
- repetitive
- subjective
- difficult to scale
- vulnerable to human bias
- disconnected from measurable product decisions

InsightFlow AI must automate this workflow.

27. SQL DEMONSTRATION
==================================================

Include a page or expandable section showing representative SQL queries.

Example query:

SELECT
    primary_theme,
    COUNT(*) AS mentions,
    ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(*) / SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 2) AS share
FROM reviews
GROUP BY primary_theme
ORDER BY mentions DESC;

Example query:

SELECT
    app_version,
    COUNT(*) AS total_reviews,
    AVG(rating) AS avg_rating,
    SUM(CASE WHEN sentiment = 'Negative' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS negative_reviews
FROM reviews
GROUP BY app_version
ORDER BY negative_reviews DESC;
